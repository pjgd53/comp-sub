🖼️ Page Setup and Borders

Change the shape of your page and add a frame — great for posters and certificates.

↕️ Portrait or Landscape?

The way your page faces is called its orientation. Go to the Layout tab and click Orientation.

Portrait
(tall)
Letters, reports
Landscape
(wide)
Posters, certificates

📏 Margins

Margins are the empty spaces around the edges of the page. Word won't type in them, so your words don't fall off the paper when you print. Click Layout › Margins and choose Normal for most work, or Narrow if you need more room.

Normal
Narrow
The dashed box shows where you can type. Narrow margins give you more space.

🎨 Page Color and Page Borders

Click the Design tab. On the right side you'll find:

  • Page Color — changes the background color of the whole page.
  • Page Borders — opens a window where you can add a frame around the page.
