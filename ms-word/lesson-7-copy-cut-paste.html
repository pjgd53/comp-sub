📋 Copy, Cut, and Paste

Three shortcuts that save you from typing the same thing twice.

🖱️ First, Select the Text

Before you can copy or move words, you have to select them — just like in Lesson 3. Click and drag across the words so they turn highlighted. To select everything in the document at once, press Ctrl + A (A for "All").

The Clipboard group on the Home tab has the Cut ✂️, Copy ⧉ and Paste 📋 buttons.

⌨️ The Three Magic Shortcuts

Ctrl + C
Copy — makes a copy. The words stay where they are.
Ctrl + X
Cut — takes the words out so you can move them.
Ctrl + V
Paste — puts the copied or cut words where your cursor is.

Copied or cut words wait in a hidden place called the Clipboard until you paste them. You can paste the same thing again and again!

🔀 Moving a Sentence (Cut + Paste)

  1. Select the sentence you want to move.
  2. Press Ctrl + X — it disappears (don't worry, it's on the Clipboard).
  3. Click where you want it to go.
  4. Press Ctrl + V — there it is!
Oops! Cut or pasted something by mistake? Press Ctrl + Z to Undo, just like you learned in Lesson 2.
Try It! — Fix the Mixed-Up Story Type these sentences in this order:
"Then she ate breakfast. Maria woke up early. Finally, she walked to school."
Use Cut and Paste to put them in the right order. Then Copy the word "Maria" and paste it at the end to add a new sentence about her.

✅ What You Learned

  • Select text first — or press Ctrl + A to select everything.
  • Ctrl + C copies, Ctrl + X cuts, Ctrl + V pastes.
  • Cut + Paste moves words; Copy + Paste repeats them.
  • Ctrl + Z undoes a mistake.
