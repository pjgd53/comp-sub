🖼️ Adding Pictures and Printing

How to put an image into your document and get your finished work onto paper.

📷 Inserting a Picture

Pictures make a document more fun and easier to understand. To add one, click the Insert tab, then click Pictures. Word will let you choose a picture file from your computer, and it will drop into your document right where your cursor was.

The small white squares around a picture's edges are called handles — you'll see these once a picture is selected.

📐 Resizing a Picture

Once a picture is in your document, click it once to select it — small square handles appear around its edges. To make the picture bigger or smaller, click and drag one of the corner handles. Dragging a corner keeps the picture's shape looking right; dragging an edge handle can stretch or squash it, so corners are usually the safest choice.

Try It! After inserting a picture, click somewhere else in your document first to deselect it — you'll notice the handles disappear. Click the picture again any time you want to resize or move it.

🖨️ Printing Your Document

When your document is ready, you can print it onto paper. Press Ctrl + P (or click File, then Print) to open the Print screen. Here you can choose which printer to use and how many copies you want, and see a Print Preview — a picture of exactly how your pages will look before any paper is used.

Always check the Print Preview before printing — it saves paper if something needs fixing first!
Remember Printing uses paper and ink, so it's a good habit to re-read your document and check the preview carefully before you click Print — especially if you need more than one copy!

✅ What You Learned

  • The Insert tab's Pictures button adds an image to your document.
  • Click a picture to select it, then drag a corner handle to resize it neatly.
  • Ctrl + P opens the Print screen, where you pick a printer and number of copies.
  • Always check the Print Preview before printing to save paper.
