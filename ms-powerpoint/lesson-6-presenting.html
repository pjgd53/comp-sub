🎤 Presenting Your Slideshow

Your slides are ready — now it’s time to show them to your class!

▶️ Starting the Slide Show

F5
Start from the first slide
Shift + F5
Start from the slide you are on now
Esc
End the slide show

You can also click the Slide Show tab and click From Beginning. Your slide fills the whole screen, and transitions and animations play.

⏭️ Moving Between Slides

Next ➡️

Click the mouse, or press →, Space, or Enter.

⬅️ Back

Press ← or Backspace to go to the previous slide or animation.

Each click plays the next animation first. When all animations on a slide are done, the next click goes to the next slide.

🌟 Tips for a Great Presentation

  • Practice at least once before presenting to the class.
  • Face your audience — glance at the screen, but don't read every word from it.
  • Speak loudly and slowly so everyone can hear.
  • Use the Notes area for reminders of what to say.
  • End with a "Thank you!" slide and ask if anyone has questions.
Try It! — Final Project Finish your All About Me presentation with at least 5 slides: a title slide, at least one picture per slide, one transition for all slides, and one animation on each slide. Add a Thank You slide at the end, practice, and present it to a classmate using F5!

✅ What You Learned

  • F5 starts from the beginning; Shift + F5 starts from the current slide.
  • Click, →, Space, or Enter go forward; ← goes back.
  • Esc ends the slide show.
  • Practice, face your audience, and speak clearly.
