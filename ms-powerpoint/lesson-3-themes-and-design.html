🎨 Themes and Slide Design

Give your whole presentation a matching look — and make it your own.

🎨 What Is a Theme?

A theme is a ready-made design. With one click it gives every slide the same colors, fonts, and background, so your presentation looks neat and matching from start to end.

▶️ Try it — click a theme:
My Favorite Animals by Juan Dela Cruz · Grade 5
Notice how the background, colors, and font all change together. That's a theme!

🖱️ Applying a Theme

  1. Click the Design tab.
  2. Point your mouse at a theme (don't click yet). Your slide shows a preview of how it will look.
  3. Click the theme you like. It changes all your slides.
  4. Click the small More ▾ arrow at the side of the gallery to see even more themes.

Want a different theme on just one slide? Select that slide, then right-click the theme and choose Apply to Selected Slides.

🌈 Variants: Colors, Fonts, and Background Styles

Next to the themes is the Variants group. Each variant is the same theme in different colors. Click the More ▾ arrow in Variants to change things one by one:

🎨 Colors — pick a different set of colors for the whole theme.
🔤 Fonts — change the font for all titles and body text at once.
🖼️ Background Styles — choose a lighter or darker background.

🖌️ Format Background

You can design your own background too! Click Design › Format Background. A panel opens on the right with these choices:

Solid Fill — one color
Gradient Fill — colors blending
🏝️
Picture Fill — a photo
Pattern Fill — stripes, dots

The background only changes the slide you are on. Click Apply to All at the bottom of the panel to use it on every slide.

📐 Slide Size

▭ Widescreen (16:9)

The normal size today. Fits TVs, projectors, and laptops.

▢ Standard (4:3)

Almost square. Used for older projectors or printing.

Change it at Design › Slide Size. It's best to choose the size before you add pictures, so nothing gets stretched.

💡 Designer (Design Ideas) In PowerPoint for Microsoft 365, the Designer button on the Design tab suggests ready-made layouts for your slide, especially after you add a picture. Just click one you like! (Older versions and other apps may not have this button.)
Make It Easy to Read! Use dark text on a light background or light text on a dark background. Avoid busy pictures behind your words, and keep one theme for the whole presentation.
Try It! — Design Your All About Me Open your All About Me presentation. 1) Apply a theme you like. 2) Try two different Variants and keep your favorite. 3) Change the theme Fonts. 4) Give only your title slide a Gradient Fill background. Can everyone still read your words?

✅ What You Learned

  • A theme gives all slides matching colors, fonts, and background — find them on the Design tab.
  • Variants change the theme’s Colors, Fonts, and Background Styles.
  • Format Background lets you use a solid color, gradient, picture, or pattern — click Apply to All for every slide.
  • Slide Size: Widescreen (16:9) or Standard (4:3).
  • Always check that your text is easy to read.
