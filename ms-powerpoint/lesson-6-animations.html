✨ Animations

Bring the things on your slide to life — one at a time.

✨ Transition or Animation?

🔀 Transition

Moves the whole slide. It happens between slides.

✨ Animation

Moves one thing on a slide — a title, a picture, or a line of text.

🛠️ Adding an Animation

  1. Click the thing you want to animate — a picture or a text box.
  2. Click the Animations tab.
  3. Click an effect, like Fly In. A small number 1 appears next to the object — that's the order it will play.
  4. Animate another object and it gets number 2, and so on.

🎨 The Four Kinds of Animation

🟢 Entrance
Makes something appear (Fade, Fly In, Zoom).
🟡 Emphasis
Makes something already there stand out (Pulse, Spin, Grow).
🔴 Exit
Makes something disappear (Fly Out, Fade Out).
🔵 Motion Path
Makes something travel along a line across the slide.

⏱️ When Does It Start?

On Click — plays when you click the mouse. This is the default.
With Previous — plays at the same time as the animation before it.
After Previous — plays right after the one before it finishes.

Open the Animation Pane to see all the animations on a slide in order, and drag them up or down to change the order.

Don’t Overdo It! Animations should help your audience follow along — for example, showing one bullet point at a time. If everything spins and bounces, people watch the effects instead of listening to you.
Try It! On one of your All About Me slides, give the title a Fade entrance and the picture a Fly In entrance. Set the picture to After Previous, then click Preview.

✅ What You Learned

  • Animations move things on a slide; transitions move whole slides.
  • Select an object, then pick an effect on the Animations tab. Numbers show the order.
  • Four kinds: Entrance (green), Emphasis (yellow), Exit (red), Motion Path.
  • Start options: On Click, With Previous, After Previous.
