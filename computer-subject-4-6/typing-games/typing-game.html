How are WPM and Accuracy counted?
  • Press Space to move to the next word. Pressed Space too early, or spotted a mistake? Press Backspace on an empty box to go back and fix the previous word.
  • WPM = letters in words you typed correctly (plus their spaces) ÷ 5, per minute. Wrong words don't count.
  • Raw WPM counts every letter you typed, right or wrong.
  • Accuracy = correct key presses ÷ all key presses. A mistake still counts even if you fix it with Backspace — so type carefully!
