⌨️ Typing and Saving Your Work

How to start a new document, type your ideas, fix mistakes, and keep your work safe.

🆕 Starting a New Document

When you open Word, you usually get a blank white page ready for you to type on. The blinking cursor starts at the top-left corner — that's where your first letter will appear.

⌨️ Typing Basics

As you press keys, letters appear where the cursor is, and the cursor moves along with them. A few keys are especially useful to know:

Pressing Enter starts a new line, just like starting a new sentence on paper.

Spacebar adds a space between words. Enter moves the cursor down to start a new line or paragraph. Backspace deletes the letter just before the cursor — handy for fixing typos.

↩️ Undo and Redo

Made a mistake? You don't have to fix it by hand. Word remembers your last actions, so you can press Ctrl + Z (or click the curved back-arrow at the top of the screen) to undo your last change. If you undo something by accident, Ctrl + Y (or the forward-curved arrow) will redo it.

Try It! Type a short sentence, then press Ctrl+Z a few times and watch your words disappear backwards! Press Ctrl+Y to bring them back.

💾 Saving Your Document

Typing your work is only half the job — if you close Word without saving, your work can disappear! Saving stores your document as a file on the computer so you can open it again later, even after turning the computer off.

The fastest way to save is pressing Ctrl + S on your keyboard. The first time you save a brand-new document, Word will ask you to choose a file name and a location to save it — this is called Save As.

Give your file a name you'll remember — Word automatically adds ".docx" to the end.

After that first save, pressing Ctrl + S again just quietly updates the same file — no dialog box needed. That's why it's a good habit to save often while you work, especially before you take a break!

Remember If the power goes out or the computer freezes, any typing you haven't saved yet could be lost. Save early, and save often!

✅ What You Learned

  • The cursor shows where your next typed letter will appear.
  • Spacebar, Enter, and Backspace are your everyday typing tools.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes a mistake; Ctrl+Z then Ctrl+Y redoes it.
  • Ctrl+S saves your work — the first time, you'll choose a file name (Save As).
  • Save often, so you never lose your work!
